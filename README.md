## 🚀 Features

* **⚡ Blazing Fast & Lightweight:** Optimized for speed by removing heavy, unstable chart dependencies. Enjoy instant portfolio loading, sending, and swapping without any API limits or lag.
* **💳 Buy Crypto (Fiat On-Ramp):** Seamlessly fund your wallet via credit card (MoonPay, Transak) or get directed to top-tier, low-fee exchanges (MEXC, Gate.io).
* **🔗 WalletConnect & QR Scanner:** Instantly connect and interact with your favorite dApps across the Solana ecosystem securely.
* **🔥 Ultimate Burn Tool:** Easily clean up your wallet! Burn annoying spam NFTs or empty token accounts to reclaim your SOL storage rent safely (includes clear warnings for unsupported cNFTs).
* **🔄 Secure Swap:** Powered by Jupiter Aggregator.
    * **Pay With (From):** Supports swapping **from** any token supported by Jupiter.
    * **Receive (To):** For enhanced security, swaps are limited **to a strictly verified list of 44 selected tokens**.
* **🥩 Liquid Staking:** Integrated with Jito (JitoSOL). Earn staking rewards effortlessly.
* **🛡️ Non-Custodial:** User owns the private keys (encrypted locally).
* **🕵️ Privacy:** No tracking, no personal data collection.
* **📲 NFC Touch Payments (Android Only):** Send and receive SOL or tokens instantly just by tapping two RamyaWallet devices together—no need to scan or copy addresses. Bringing seamless, real-world payment experiences to crypto.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Framework:** React Native (TypeScript)
* **Blockchain:** Solana (Web3.js)
* **Key Integrations:** Jupiter Aggregator, Helius, WalletConnect
